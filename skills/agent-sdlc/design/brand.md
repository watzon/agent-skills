# Brand, logo, and mascot

Read when a product has no brand and you are at step 6 of the [design process](index.md).

**Round 2: not written yet.** Planned topics:

- Deriving brand personality and voice from the product's purpose and name.
- Positioning against neighbors, and what the brand refuses.
- Logo: concept from the name and subject, wordmark or symbol, sizes, monochrome, small-size legibility.
- Mascot: when one helps and when it hurts; consistency rules.
- Writing the brand down so later work can follow it as a system.

Until then: write one paragraph on personality and voice drawn from the purpose and name, keep any logo a simple wordmark in the chosen typeface, and add no mascot unless the brief asks for one.

## Done when

- [ ] A personality and voice paragraph exists and traces to the purpose and name.
- [ ] Any logo or mascot was asked for or is a plain wordmark.
